Why read it

Built to be read in an evening, and used the next day

Beyond Burnups is intentionally concise and practical. It's for Scrum Masters, Delivery and Project Managers, Product Owners, PMO functions, and senior leaders who are tired of presenting dates that have a low statistical chance of being right and calling it a plan. Every technique is backed by free, open-source tools you can apply to your own Jira or Azure DevOps data straight away.
